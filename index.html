            Which way do the toes point on screen?

            This gives the angle its sign: bending is positive, over-straightening (hyperextension) is negative. Filming the right leg from its outer side with the back camera, the toes point right. If bending shows a negative number, flip this.

            Install on the phone

            1. Android (Chrome): tap Install at the top, or menu ⋮ → Install app.
            2. iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.
            3. Laptop (Chrome / Edge): the install icon in the address bar.
            4. Open it from the new icon. It works offline. Readings stay on this device, so export from Records after every volunteer.

            Stickers on the leg

            1. Three colours (recommended): blue hip, pink knee, green ankle, round matt stickers, 2–3 cm. Each colour names its joint, so they can never be mixed up.
            2. Hip: the bony bump on the side of the hip; ask the volunteer to roll the leg in and out and feel it move.
            3. Knee: the bony bump on the outer knee, 1–2 cm above the joint line, on skin through the 2 cm hole in the knee cap.
            4. Ankle: the outer ankle bone.
            5. No clothing or background in any sticker colour. These are the standard clinical goniometry landmarks (Norkin & White).

            Camera

            1. Side-on, square to the plane the knee bends in. This is the only placement rule that changes accuracy.
            2. 2–3 m away, phone at knee height, leg in the middle and filling most of the picture height.
            3. 1× zoom, even light, plain background. Phone rotation, height and distance do not change the angle.

            What this measures

            2D marker-based video goniometry in the sagittal plane: the angle at the knee sticker between the lines to the hip and ankle stickers. Validated only with printed cards (Accuracy tab), not certified as a medical device. Photos and video stay on this device; only the numbers you save are kept.
